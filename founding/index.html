Building Tactivo with grassroots clubs

Nothing to buy. Nothing to sign. Just your coaches' honest feedback.

Grassroots football runs on volunteers: people who finish a day's work and go straight to training, for the kids. Tactivo is an app that makes that hour easier, and we want to build what comes next together with the clubs who use it.

Become a Tactivo Founding Club

Built for coaches, by coaches.

It's simple. Introduce us to a few of your coaches. They use Tactivo, and every few weeks they tell us:

  • What's working
  • What isn't
  • What they'd add
  • What would make their week easier

We build from what they tell us, and your club sees it first.

Every feature from here on will be shaped by the clubs we work with. We'd love yours to be one of them.
