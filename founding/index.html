Building Tactivo with grassroots clubs

Grassroots football runs on volunteers: people who finish a day's work and go straight to training, for the kids. Tactivo is a free app that makes that hour easier, and we want to build what comes next together with the clubs who use it.

What it does today

Real screens from the app
A session ready in minutes
Drills you can see, with the coaching points
Shared with the other coaches at your club

What we're building with clubs

CONCEPT · NOT IN THE APP YET
An AI coaching assistant you talk to after training. It takes the notes.
Coaching cards made automatically from those notes. No extra work for coaches.

Become a Tactivo Founding Club

Built for coaches, by coaches.

It's simple. Introduce us to a few of your coaches. They use Tactivo, and every few weeks they tell us:

  • What's working
  • What isn't
  • What they'd add
  • What would make their week easier

We build from what they tell us, and your club sees it first.

Every feature from here on will be shaped by the clubs we work with. We'd love yours to be one of them.
