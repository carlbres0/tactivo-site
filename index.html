A season has four corners. Tactivo plans against all of them.

  • Technical

    On the ball. Touch, passing, dribbling, finishing

  • Tactical

    Off the ball. Shape, pressing, decisions

  • Physical

    Bodies. Speed, endurance, balance

  • Psych / Social

    People. Resilience, teamwork, competing

Technical

You can see the drill before you have to explain it.

Every drill in Tactivo plays as an animated scene on a 3D pitch. Who starts where. When the pass goes. Where the ball ends up. Where the run comes from.

Which means you are not stood in the rain at ten to seven decoding a static diagram with nine children waiting on you.

Tactical

The AI planner asks two questions. Then it writes the session.

What is this session building towards, and who have you got. That is the whole interview. Tactivo then picks drills that fit the age band, orders them so the session actually flows, and sizes each one until the minutes add up.

Ask twice for the same thing and you get a different session that is still right. There is no single "U12 possession" answer to memorise.

The same focus is a different session for a different squad. Tactivo moves the grid, the numbers and the rules with the age band and the turnout, rather than handing you a drill for sixteen and wishing you luck.
