Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Tactivo account — the app it applies to is Tactivo, published by Tactivo Limited. You can do it yourself in the app, or ask us to do it for you. Both routes delete the same data.

Option 1 — delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Tactivo and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm. The account and its data are erased immediately.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

Email info@tactivo.co.uk from the address on the account, with the subject Delete my Tactivo account. Tell us the email address the account uses. We will confirm it is you, delete the account, and email you when it is done.

Request deletion by email

You can also write to us: Tactivo Limited, 63 Carlton Road, Erith, DA8 1DX, United Kingdom.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your coaching profile: display name, role, qualification, experience.
  • Your session plans, saved sessions, drills you wrote, ratings and favourites.
  • Usage and device records linked to your account.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics — kept indefinitely. These cannot identify you and are not linked to your account.
  • Records the law requires us to hold — for example transaction records for accounting, or logs needed for fraud and abuse prevention. Kept only for the period the law requires, then deleted.
  • Copies other coaches have already saved — if you shared a session publicly and another coach saved it to their own library, their copy stays with them. Your name is removed from it.

How long it takes

Your account and its data are erased from our live systems the moment you confirm. Encrypted backups age out within 30 days.

Full detail on what we hold and why is in the privacy policy. Questions: info@tactivo.co.uk.
